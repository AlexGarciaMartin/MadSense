MadSense native tactile latency — candidate A, 2026-10-31

User evidence: native effects and triggers work in Stellar Blade, but jump landing
and shooting feedback are noticeably late. This supersedes any inference from
successful packets alone; host HID input latency is not tactile output latency.

Confirmed delays in source before candidate A:
1. Every Bluetooth lane start sent8silent combined packets (10.667ms each) before
   reading native haptics at all: about85ms of deliberate initial haptic gating.
   This repeated after an idle gap longer than150ms, not merely at app launch.
2. Native queue read oldest64bytes first, then discarded one extra packet only
   when more than3packets remained. Sustained backlog therefore targeted roughly
   32ms and a large burst could take several late packets to catch up.
3. Queue capacity2048bytes represents341ms at3000stereo frames/sec. Capacity is
   a worst-case storage bound, NOT proof of measured typical latency.

Candidate A changes:
- Speaker Opus remains silent for8warmup packets; strict native PCM is sent in
  the first warmup report. No cadence/report ID/prime sequence modification.
- Before choosing a native packet, retain only newest128bytes (64stereo frames,
  21.333ms of content), discarding whole stereo samples from older backlog.
  Read64bytes, leaving at most one packet. Underflow pads silence; finite native
  impulses are consumed once and do not get looped.
- Speaker buffer/catch-up and legacy audio-derived path are unchanged, isolating
  the native haptic timing change for subjective A/B comparison.

Validation:63/63 relevant tests passed (native-low-latency.trx). New tests prove
the first warmup packet carries a48-frame or512-frame native impulse, all later
warmup packets are silent after consumption, and a6-block burst starts with
block5 then6 instead of replaying blocks1..4. This is SOFTWARE behavior with a
fake transport, not physical actuator latency or quality certification.

Tradeoffs/remaining questions:
- Backlog trimming sacrifices old waveform detail rather than reproduce it late.
  If upstream delivers big bursts normally, this can shorten effects. Candidate A
  must be tried in Stellar Blade for intensity/continuity as well as timing.
- A128byte queue limit bounds buffered CONTENT duration, not end-to-end latency
  or absolute sample age after a paused process. The USB/game/Bluetooth/controller
  queues and rendering remain outside that bound.
- Whether firmware honors native PCM during Opus warmup must be physically
  validated, especially an isolated effect after a long silence. Packet success
  alone cannot prove this. Independent nativePCM/audio protocol lanes support
  the experiment but do not replace a hardware observation.
- USB/IP OUT is FIFO, processed1packet per USB frame with catch-up on late timer
  callbacks. No fixed100ms userland capture delay exists in the direct endpoint
  path. Pending URB depth and arrival-to-delivery age are not yet instrumented.
  Do not bypass/reorder USB scheduling without measuring that queue first.
- DSX log queue size256bytes/depth5 cannot be converted into tactile latency
  without knowing the actual data format, endpoint and completion policy.

Primary protocol reference read:
https://github.com/hbashton/DS4Windows/blob/main/docs/dualsense-bluetooth-audio-haptics.md
It separates native haptics (3kHz signed stereo PCM packet0x12) from controller

speaker audio. It does not establish DSX latency or this firmware's warmup need.
